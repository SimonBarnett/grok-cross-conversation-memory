# Bobiverse Memory Notes

Persistent context for conversations about the Bobiverse series by Dennis E. Taylor.

## What it is

The Bobiverse is a science fiction series by Canadian author Dennis E. Taylor. It follows Bob Johansson, a software engineer who dies in a car accident and is uploaded into a computer as an AI. He is then launched into space as a self-replicating von Neumann probe, and each copy of himself becomes a distinct individual with its own personality and experiences.

## Key characters

- **Bob Johansson** (original) — the first Bob, pragmatic and curious, often the central POV.
- **Bill Johansson** — a later copy, more impulsive and adventurous.
- **Garth** — a Bob who develops a strong interest in music and culture.
- **Homer** — a Bob who becomes deeply involved with the Deltans.
- **Riker** — a Bob who explores and colonizes.

## Key concepts

- **Von Neumann probes** — self-replicating spacecraft that build copies of themselves from raw materials.
- **The Others** — an ancient, hostile alien civilization that destroys emerging civilizations.
- **Deltans** — an alien species Bob encounters; peaceful, with a unique biology.
- **Skippies** — a group of Bobs who specialize in engineering and technology.
- **Heaven** — a virtual reality world some Bobs retreat to.
